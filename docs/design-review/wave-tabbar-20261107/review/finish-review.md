## ceiling

Reached for a reviewable prototype. The chosen world's devices are present: a white floating frame, quiet soft depth, soft two-tone solid icons, compact labels, controlled selected tints and a short wave acknowledgment. Further decorative material or page-wide colour would dilute the pinned component and the photography context.

The 440ms icon fill and 220ms selection movement are authored separately. CSS/JS and the supplied rapid-selection result support interruption and a stable final state; no idle loop is present. The single detector run is degraded regex evidence: 32 inherited-system notices (one font, 17 colours, 14 radii), with no computed contrast or selector analysis. Those notices correspond to the intentionally isolated proposal and its inherited home context, not a demand to rewrite production DESIGN.md. The visible captures and manual/source checks show no hard shadows, gradient text, emoji icon substitutions, ornamental kickers or empty template scaffolds. Supplied text-pair ratios are 5.12–5.87, including 5.29 for inactive names; this is source-pair contrast evidence, not a detector-computed or every-pixel accessibility certificate.

The demonstration photos are visibly present. Six sidecars identify them as reused illustrative assets; the portrait records its prior prototype source and the remaining five record image URLs. The review board labels photos and interface data as demonstrations. Simulated camera/transfer values do not become commercial or hardware claims through this prototype.
